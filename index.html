Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Information We Collect

BLL Athletics may collect information that users provide when creating or using an account, including names, email addresses, team information, player profile information, wellness information, messages, schedules, and other content entered into the application.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and improve BLL Athletics, operate team management features, support communication, manage schedules, provide wellness and player development functionality, maintain account security, and respond to support requests.

Service Providers

BLL Athletics may use third-party service providers to operate portions of the application, including services used for authentication, database storage, notifications, application infrastructure, and related functionality. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services.

Data Sharing

BLL Athletics does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the application, comply with legal obligations, protect users or the service, or when a user directs or authorizes the sharing.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate operational records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Account and Data Deletion

Users may request account or personal data deletion by contacting BLL Athletics Support at bllathleticssupport@gmail.com . Requests will be reviewed and processed subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to help protect user information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

BLL Athletics is intended for authorized members of athletic programs. Organizations using the application are responsible for ensuring appropriate authorization and consent when required by applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or requests related to personal information can be sent to bllathleticssupport@gmail.com .
